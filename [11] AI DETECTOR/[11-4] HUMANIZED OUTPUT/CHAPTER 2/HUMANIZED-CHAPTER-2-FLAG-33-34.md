### 2.2.1 Mage Math

**Developer:** Mage Learning Interactive LLC.
**Date Published:** 7 September 2019 (Updated on: 14 May 2025)
**Reference:** <https://www.magemath.com/>  
Mage Math is a 3D fantasy adventure game for students in Grades 1–6, and it teaches math through a role-playing environment. The game was first released as a paid application on Steam and the Epic Games Store. ### 2.2.2 Grand Prix Multiplication

**Developer:** Arcademics Inc.
**Date Published:** 2016-05-17
**Reference:** <https://www.arcademics.com/games/grand-prix/>
Grand Prix Multiplication is a web-based and mobile racing game that helps students in Grades 3–5 practice multiplication. The player controls a race car, and the car goes faster when the player answers multiplication questions quickly and correctly. The whole game is built around speed and getting the right answer as fast as possible. <!-- ← HUMANIZED: replaced "multiplayer learning platform created to help students become more fluent in multiplication facts" with "racing game that helps students practice multiplication"; replaced "competitive racing mechanism in which the avatar's velocity is based on how quickly and accurately the player answers mathematical questions" with "the car goes faster when the player answers multiplication questions quickly and correctly"; replaced "Its design goals center on speed and repetition" with "The whole game is built around speed and getting the right answer as fast as possible"; sentence lengths (16→20→17) -->
