Explain how Chronicles of Arithmos solves this by making math calculations the direct driver of combat actions and damage calculations.

The Project Objectives (1.2): Present the General Objective (to design and develop Chronicles of Arithmos with Dynamic Math Combat and Adaptive Scaling). Walk through the 12 Specific Objectives sequentially, showing how each module satisfies the SMART criteria (What + How + Result).
