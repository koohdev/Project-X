4.2.4.2 Cost Recovery Scheme

The proponents will personally fund the total development cost of ₱16,708.00 as part of their academic requirements. Since the proposed title is not a commercial product, the proponents will not recover the cost through sales or fees. The application will instead be offered for free to students and teachers to support arithmetic learning. The five-year hosting plan included in the budget will keep the system accessible after the development period without additional expense.
