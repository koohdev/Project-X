# Appendix B: Definition of Terms

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)**. Refers to the technology used in the proposed system to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as analyzing data and generating recommendations.

**Database**. Refers to an organized collection of data used by the proposed system to store, manage, and retrieve information efficiently.

**Information System**. Refers to the combination of people, processes, hardware, software, and data used to collect, process, store, and provide information.

**User Interface**. Refers to the visual elements and controls through which users interact with the proposed system.

**Web Application**. Refers to a software application that can be accessed through a web browser and is designed to perform specific functions for its intended users.


## Guidelines for Definition of Terms

**Include only important terms**. Define terms that are technical, unfamiliar, specialized, or frequently used in the study.

**Define terms based on the study**. The definition should explain how the term is used within your specific project, rather than simply copying a dictionary definition.

**Arrange terms alphabetically**. List the terms from A to Z.

**Use the term as the heading**. The term should be clearly distinguished from its definition.Example:Database. An organized collection of data...

**Keep definitions concise**. Avoid lengthy explanations. Generally, one to three sentences should be sufficient.

**Avoid circular definitions**. Do not define a term by simply repeating the term itself.

❌ Database. A database is a system for databases.✅ Database. An organized collection of data that allows information to be stored, managed, and retrieved.

**Avoid unnecessary technical jargon**. Use simple and understandable language whenever possible.

Use operational definitions when appropriate. For Capstone Projects, prioritize explaining what the term means and how it functions in the proposed system.

**Do not include every technical term**. Only include terms that are relevant and necessary for understanding the study.

**Be consistent**. Use the same terminology throughout the entire manuscript. If you use "Administrator" in the Definition of Terms, avoid switching between "Admin," "System Administrator," and "Administrator" unless they refer to different roles.

**Avoid unsupported claims**. If a definition is based on a specific source or technical standard, provide the appropriate citation according to the required citation style.

Review the list after completing the manuscript. Make sure that the terms defined are actually used in the study and that important technical terms introduced in later chapters are also considered.
