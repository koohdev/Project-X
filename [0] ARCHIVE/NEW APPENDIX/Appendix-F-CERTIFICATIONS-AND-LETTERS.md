## GUIDELINES FOR CERTIFICATIONS AND LETTERS

**Include only relevant documents**. Every certification or letter must have a direct connection to the Capstone Project.

Use the official project title consistently. The title appearing in certifications and letters must match the approved Capstone Project title.

**Obtain the required signatures**. Documents requiring approval, validation, or certification must be properly signed by the appropriate person.

**Include dates**. All certifications, letters, and evaluation documents should indicate the date of issuance or activity when applicable.

Do not fabricate signatures or certifications. Students must only include documents that were actually issued, signed, or authorized.

**Use consistent names**. Names of students, advisers, evaluators, clients, and organizations should be spelled consistently throughout the manuscript.

**Maintain chronological or logical order**. Arrange documents according to their purpose and sequence in the Capstone process.

Include scanned copies of signed documents when required. Scanned documents must be clear and readable.

**Do not alter signed documents**. Do not modify the content, signatures, dates, or other information in an official document after it has been signed.
